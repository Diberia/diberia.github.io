Diberian Mythology

The Diberian mythology is based heavily on Germanic and Norse mythology. That is because I think the mythology of a culture cannot be that easily replaced by migration. Therefore I only "customised" these mythologies so that they fit into the Diberian culture. Keep in mind that the "Mythology" part of this project is more or less a "customization" of Germanic and Norse mythology. Yet still the reason why I did this is because of the fact that I suppose Diberians and Germanic people were once one people before Indo-European languages and cultures spread, therefore Germanic and Norse mythology are a perfect fit.

The Scripture

The "Scripture of the germanic gods" is the most one of the most important parts of Diberian mythology. Though it does not describe the "Germanic" gods but rather the "Diberian" gods, it still explains much about their belief. The reason why it is called "... of the germanic gods" is because it was written down by Christian Scholars somewhere after 400CE based on oral traditions of Diberians. That is also the reason for why the scriptures original form is in latin.

(Of course this scripture is entirely constructed by me and therefore please keep in mind that I DO NOT know latin and used machine translation for the latin version)

Scriptura Deorum Germanicorum

I
Dii divina naturae elementa sunt.
Quamquam nos nostros habemus, aliae fortasse gentes alios habent.
"Alia fortasse civilitas erunt."
Post mortem, expergisci potes ut alius ex genere humano.
Aut, si dii tibi favent, in ipsa deorum aula locum invenire.
Sicut omnibus animantibus in mundo contingit.
Sed vita non potest fieri nisi eius generis quod ipsa antea fuit.

Vis Primordialis est locus ubi omnia inter se coniunguntur, vis divina, diis superior. Sed nihil est
quod animam habeat. Omnia est, nusquam tamen invenitur. Hic nunc est, semperque hic fuit, sed
numquam hic fuit. Vicini nostri eam Yggdrasil vocant, arborem.

Primus omnium entium, qui non humanae originis fuit, Imar appellatus est.
Imar superbia plenus omnes vitae formas crudeliter necavit, atque ex sanguine eorum furor belli
ortus est.

Cum homines ex sordidis creaturis, quarum bracchia cruribus longiora erant, creati essent, familiae
atque stirpes (gentes) eis datae sunt.
Forma sua purissima nobiles erant, omnium colorum atque varietatum.
Sed cum Mater Caelestis animum alio avertisset, deus belli mortisque mendacia de regno suo
disseminavit.
"Bonum est", inquit, "atque intrare potestis, si omnia feceritis quae Pater Caelestis vetuit."
Cum autem Mater Caelestis ad homines respexisset, fracta est.

"Quomodo creatio nostra perfecta tam crudelis esse potest?" lacrimans interrogavit, in mariti sui
ulnas cadens.
Pater Caelestis tantum in eius correctione occupatus erat.
Deus sapientiae, qui coniugibus amicissimus erat, fortiter id conatus est, sed humanitas tantum crudelior facta est.
Mater Caelestis tristis erat, eiusque lacrimae usque ad hunc diem in terram pluunt.
Sed Vis Primordialis consilium diis dono dare potuit.
Quinque stirpes, divino sanguine ortae, intellexerunt bellum tantum ultimum remedium esse debere, non primum honorem.
Ex eo tempore dii varias post mortem sortes constituerunt:

1. Regnum Dei Mortis "Godeim"
Sceleratis atque hominibus omnium pessimis destinatum.

2. Regnum Deae Mortis "Skapania"
Hominibus malis qui veniam petunt destinatum.

3. Campi Matris Caelestis "Nilfen"
Hominibus mediocribus destinati.

4. Aulae Dei Sapientiae "Vahal'ha"
Hominibus bonis destinatae.

5. Vita aeterna inter deos
Heroibus destinata.

6. Renascentia
Via omnibus libera, in qua nova condicio tua ex vita priore pendet.

Sed dii etiam haec verba dixerunt, verba quae determinant qui esse debeas et qui desinere:
"Vos ipsi vobis auxilium ferte, qui calamitate perditi estis, nam tum dii quoque vobis auxilium ferre possunt.
Sed initium novum petere potestis."
Sed quid? Quid nos post mortem exspectat?

Godeim mundus doloris et ignis est, crudelis atque obscurus.

Skapania mundus cinereus est, sed non omnino malus, nam paenitentiam egisti.

Nilfen campus mitis est, mundo in quo nunc vivitis similis.

Vahal'ha locus divitiarum, gaudii atque tranquillitatis est.
Hic boni homines frui possunt.

Renascentia omnibus libera est. Attamen vita quam vixisti statuet qualem vitam victurus sis.

Et dii monuerunt verba Vis Primordialis litteris Germanicis (litteris Diberianis) scripta nimiam
potestatem in mundo habere, atque propterea scribenda vitanda esse, nisi in armilla tua.

II
Vis Primordialis, Dii et Serpens

Tria divina naturae elementa.
Nam vis sunt, ens et tempus.
Vis Primordialis centrum omnis materiae, spatii atque animarum est.
Dii sunt entia quae cum Vi Primordiali agunt, quae eis potestatem divinam tribuit.
Serpens autem, quoniam circa Vim Primordialem convolutus est, caudam suam persequens, tempus est.
Serpens finem Vis Primordialis exspectat, dum tempus in unam directionem vertit atque inimicos in vita post mortem devorat.
Quamquam Vis Primordialis atque dii contra eum saeviunt, ad concordiam tamen pervenerunt.
Serpentem ut alium deum colite, atque ille, Serpens, curabit ut a diis, non ab ipso, iudicemini.
Inimicos enim persequetur, quamquam in aulis propriarum religionum refugium invenire possunt.

Cum autem tempus procederet, deus libertatis celeriter intellexit Serpentem neque ossibus cedere neque tempus cuiquam cedere.
Itaque deus libertatis Serpenti amicus factus est, atque ex eo tempore tempus solutum est.

Cum tempus volvi pergeret, Mater Caelestis unum ultimum honorem praestitit.
Goðasko placare conata est, ut homines in terra maiorem misericordiam haberent; ille tamen oculum eius sinistrum eruit.
Atque ex eo die dextrum tantum oculum retinet, sinistrum autem absentem numquam tegit, ut signum probationis atque sacrificii sui.

Scripture of the germanic gods (English version)

I
The gods are the divine elements of nature.
Though we have ours, others perhaps have others.
"Another civilization they may be."
After death, you may awaken as another one of humans kin.
Or, if the gods favor you, end up in the hall of gods yourself.
As it is for all life in the world.
Though a life can only become the kin of what it once was.

The Urkraft is where everything connects, a divine power,
higher than the gods. But it is no being. It is everything but
nowhere to be found. It is here now and always was here but
it was also never here. Our neighbors call it Yggdrasil, the tree.

The first of all beings, of non human origin, was called Imar.
Proudly Imar cruelly killed all lifeforms and of whichs blood the
rage of war was made.

As the humans were made out of dirty beings with arms longer than
their legs, they were given families and legacies (Ethnicities).
In their purest form they were noble and of all colors and variations.
But as the Sky Mother was distracted, the god of war and death,
he spread lies about his realm. "It is good", he said, "and you may enter
by doing everything the Sky Father forbid you.".
And as the Sky Mother turned back to humans, she was broken.

"How can our perfect creation be so cruel?", she asked weeping,
falling into her husbands arms. The Sky Father was only obsessed with fixing it.
The god of wisdom, a close friend to the couple, he tried it with boldness, but
humanity only became more cruel. The Sky Mother was sad, and her tears
rain down to earth until this day. But the Urkraft was able to gift the gods an
idea. Five bloodlines, descended by the divine, understood that war,
war shall only be the last tool and not the first honor.
From that point on the gods divided the possibilities after death into

1. Realm of the god of death "Godeim"
For villains and the most evil of mankind

2. Realm of the goddess of death "Skapania"
For bad people who seek forgiveness

3. The fields of the Sky Mother "Nilfen"
For average people

4. The halls of the god of wisdom "Vahal'ha"
For good people

5. A life, for eternity, among the gods
For Heroes

6. Rebirth
Optional path for everyone, your status then depends on your previous life

But the gods also said, words which shape who to be and who to cease,
"For those lost in disaster, help yourselves, for then the gods may help you.
But you can ask for a start."

But what? What shall await us after death?

Godeim is a world of pain and fire, cruel and dark.

Skapania is a world of greyness but not overall bad, for you have repented.

Nilfen is a mild field like the world you live on now.

Vahal'ha is a place of wealth, joy and calmness.
Good people may enjoy it here.

Rebirth is optional, for everyone. Yet the life you have lived
will decide which life you will live.

And the gods have warned, that the Urkrafts word in
Germanic script (Diberian script), has too much power
for the world and shall therefore be avoided to be written,
unless on your Armring.

II
The Urkraft, the gods and the Serpent.

The three divine elements of nature.
For they are force, being and time.
The Urkraft is the center of all matter, space and souls.
The gods are beings which interact with the Urkraft, which
gives them divine power. But the Serpent, as he is encrawled
around the Urkraft, chasing its own tail, is time.
The Serpent waits for the Urkraft to end while spinning time to one
direction and devouring the enemies in the afterlife.
Though the Urkraft and the gods rage against him, they've come to
an arrangement. Worship times Serpent as another god and he,
the Serpent, will make sure that you will be judged by the gods
and not him. For the enemies will be chased by him, though they may
find shelter in their own beliefs halls.

And as time moved on, the god of freedom, quickly saw that the Serpent
did not bow to bones, neither did time bow to some.
And so the god of freedom made friends with the Serpent and from
that time on, time was unbound.

As time curled on, the Sky Mother made one last honor.
She tried to calm Goðasko, so that the people on earth will have
more compassion, yet he slid out her left eye.
And from that day on, she holds only her right eye, but never covers the missing one,
as a sign for her trial and sacrifice.
