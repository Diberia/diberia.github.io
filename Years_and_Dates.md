Days:
One Day is 12 Hours (Sunrise to Sunset) (Ageþa)
One Night is 12 Hours (Sunset to Sunrise) (Eguþa)
Combined they are one Day-Day (24 Hours) (Eguja)

Week-like:
5 Day-Days are one week (Ageja)
Exactly 73 weeks in one year

Year-like:
One half year is Winter to Summer (\~December 21th to \~June 21th)
As is the other half Summer to Winter (\~June 21th to \~December 21th)
One full Year (Urtja) is two half years

Once a year is complete (Sun went up and down)
Everything will reset to one except the year count (The first day, of the first week etc.)
New years typically start at December 21th because the Sun usually is at its lowest point there.
